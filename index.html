📐 Mathematical Framework & Methodology

Optimization formulation & dispersion mechanics

1. Maximal Coverage Location Problem (MCLP) Maximizes covered population exposure \(\sum_{i} w_i y_i\) subject to budget constraint \(\sum_j c_j x_j \le B\) and radius \(d_{ij} \le R\).
2. Gaussian Plume Dispersion Predicts downwind concentration \(C(x,y) = \frac{Q}{2\pi u \sigma_y \sigma_z} \exp\left(-\frac{y^2}{2\sigma_y^2}\right)\) across urban micro-climates.
3. Health Burden & BCR Valuation Computes avoided hospitalizations and healthcare expenditure via standard epidemiological relative risk models (RR = 1.08 per 10 µg/m³ PM2.5).
